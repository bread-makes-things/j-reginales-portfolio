Polaris Music Prize

Lead Designer / Art Direction

  • Campaign branding
  • Poster design
  • Motion design

As part of aftermodern.lab, we were commissioned to design posters for the Polaris Music Prize for two consecutive years. In 2025, we created posters for two Heritage Prize winners, followed by a set of 12 posters in 2026 covering Heritage Prize winners and Album Short List nominees. Additionally, the studio was responsible for developing Polaris’ overall visual identity and marketing direction for the 2026 season.

Polaris Heritage Prize production process

One of two lead designers on a C2A awarded pair of poster designs for the 2025 Polaris Heritage Prize, and supported the project’s production through final execution.

Polaris Music Prize Short List production process

Designed the poster for Aquakultre's 1783 album, one of the ten albums shortlisted for the 2026 Polaris Album Prize and part of the set of twelve posters that aftermodern.lab was commissioned to produce. I was also part of the production team responsible for getting the entire set screen-printed. Each poster needed a minimum of fifty screen-printed copies.

Polaris Music Prize season launch

In addition to the winner posters, aftermodern.lab developed Polaris’ overall visual identity and many of its marketing assets for the 2026 season. I was particularly involved in the conceptualization and motion graphics for the season launch trailer.
