Nabua

Bi-scriptural Type Design

Nabua was my first type design assignment at OCAD University, where I attempted to create a bi-scriptural typeface in which the letterforms of both scripts influence one another throughout the design.

My plan is to continue developing this typeface over time, allowing it to evolve alongside my growing understanding of type design and bi-scriptural systems, essentially turning what started off as a school project into one that will remain forever unfinished.
